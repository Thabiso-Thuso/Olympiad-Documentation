UI Design

Brand Design Identity (The Wave)​

The core brand identity of the Olympiad Portal revolves around a custom "organic wave motif." This wave design explicitly breaks away from traditional, rigid SaaS grids to establish a bespoke brand feel. It is utilized functionally and aesthetically as a visual divider on our split-screen Authentication layouts and acts as the custom SVG edge for the off-canvas Framer Motion drawers.

Typography System​

The application uses Inter (falling back to standard system sans-serif fonts) as the global typography to maximize legibility for our data-dense dashboards.

The typographic hierarchy is structured as follows:

  • Headings: Utilize bold weights (700) to establish a clear visual hierarchy and structure.
  • Body Text & Data Tables: Utilize regular weights (400) to ensure high readability and reduce eye strain when scanning dense information.

Component Mapping & Layouts​

  • Authentication & Onboarding (/signup, /login): Utilizes a split-screen layout. One half features the brand wave and onboarding context to reinforce brand identity; the other half isolates the form inputs to reduce cognitive load and maximize conversion.
  • Organizer Dashboard: Abandons standard rigid metric cards in favor of a clean, list-based view anchored by a fixed Welcome Banner.
  • Interactive Drawers: The "Create New Olympiad" and "Add School" workflows utilize framer-motion to slide in an off-canvas side drawer, ensuring users are not redirected to a new page and maintaining their spatial context.
  • Dynamic Form Elements: Educator/Teacher input fields feature dynamic, state-driven "chips." This allows organizers to visually add, review, or remove multiple email addresses before submission.
